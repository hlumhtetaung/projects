**Statistical distribution recap**



What is distribution?

* Distribution is the measurement of how spread out the data is from the mean.
* There are four statistical moments.

  * 1st moment - mean/median			middle point of the data
  * 2nd moment - variance/standard deviation	how spread out the data is from the mean
  * 3rd moment - Skewedness			Asymmetry
  * 4th moment - Kurtosis			Weight of tail vs. mean

*\*\*Correction\*\*: Spread specifically measures \*\*variability/variance\*\*. A \*\*distribution\*\* is the overall profile or shape of the data—combining where it is centered, how spread out it is, whether it is symmetric or skewed, and if it has heavy tails.*



What does a histogram show?

* Histogram shows continuous numerical data categories that are categorized in bins.
* The diagram shows the concentration of data in bins and the spike in figure means that the respective bin have highest concentration (most common).
* The empty spaces in histogram are also important, since they shows how each bins have no population and can reveal relations.



What is a bin?

* Bins are the categories of the data ranges. They are main components for illustrating histograms. There can be empty bins that creates empty spaces (discontinuous) in histograms. These empty spaces are equally important. For example, ice cream seller can create bins on time ranges of their customer line. How many customers wait between 0-2 mins, 2-4 mins, 4-6 mins.

why is a density plot smoother than a histogram?

*\*\*Answer\*\*: A histogram draws rigid, blocky rectangles based on arbitrary bin boundaries. A density plot uses \*\*Kernel Density Estimation (KDE)\*\*, which places a smooth mini-curve (like a small Gaussian bell) over \*\*every single data point\*\*, sums them all up, and draws a continuous line. This removes the "stair-step" effect of bins.*



what are mean and median?

* Mean and median are the middle points of the data. They are the middle data points that can be used in calculating how spread out the data is.
* Mean is the arithmetic average and is heavily affected by outliers. However, median is the middle point of the sorted data, sometimes average of two middle points if the dataset is even. Median is not affected by outliers and is more robust.



what does standard deviation tell you?

* Standard deviation is used for finding out how spread out a point is from the mean. Standard deviation is the square root version of variance to eliminate squared answer and make the result as close to data as possible.



what does it mean if a distribution is skewed?

* It is asymmetrical. There are two types of skewedness -

  * Right skewed - It is also called positive skewed, where the tail is extended toward the right side.
  * Left skewed - It is also called negative skewed, where the tail is extended toward the left side.



what does a roughly normal distribution look like?

*Answer: It looks like a symmetric, smooth bell curve centered in the middle.Key Property: Mean = Median = Mode (all sit at the exact center peak).The 68–95–99.7 Rule: \~68% of data falls within $\\pm 1$ standard deviation, \~95% within $\\pm 2$, and \~99.7% within $\\pm 3$.*
